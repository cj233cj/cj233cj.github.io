[Star Wars: The Mandalorian & Grogu]:


Not bad, not excellent. The side characters feel very American, the story is bland and cartoonish, and the movie lacks a consistent motif like the dark side vs. the light side in the old Jedi films. 


However, it's still a good, safe, emotional, entertaining movie for children ...and their parents' money.
