*Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024) :
<BR> <BR> 

It is a fun movie, mostly because Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman are both perfect for their roles and carry the entire fun of it, the sexual/sarcastic/4th-wall jokes are pretty well crafted. The problem is the latter half of the movie feels like a vast cluttered toy house—it's fun here and there, with a proper accumulation of plot, but the movie ends loosely without a real villain or a timeloop paradox, something like that.
